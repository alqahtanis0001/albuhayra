## Method
Own Playwright script (Chromium from `/opt/node-tools`), one browser context per mode (390×844 mobile/touch, 1280×800 desktop; `reducedMotion: "reduce"` for the `r` modes; `locale: ar-SA`, Riyadh). Logged in through the real `/login` as أبو فهد and as سعد. Every capture runs an in-page `measure()` and records to `results.jsonl`:
- `document.scrollWidth` vs `innerWidth` (horizontal scroll);
- every visible `a/button/input/select/textarea/[role=button]` under 44 px tall;
- Eastern digits `[٠-٩]` in `body.innerText`;
- physical-side Tailwind classes (`ml- mr- pl- pr- left- right- text-left text-right rounded-l/r border-l/r float-*`) on any element;
- every fixed `[role=status]`/`[role=alert]`: box, `pointer-events`, `animation-name`, and its intersection with every visible `button/a/input/select` outside it (plus, in `dbg2/dbg3.mjs`, `elementFromPoint` at the covered controls' centres and the ✕'s own box);
- `animation-name`/`transition-duration` on `dialog[open]`, `.nav-sheet`, `.v13-paid`, `.v14-wiggle`, `#main > *`.
RTL reading order was checked by eye on the 390 and 1280 screenshots of every screen (full-page PNGs place the fixed top bar/tab bar at the scroll position of the capture — an artefact of `fullPage`, not a layout bug; the `-vp.png` files show the true viewport).

## Findings
**BLOCKER:** none.

**S1 (SHOULD) — the phone toast covers the landing page's first action row for 3 s, and its ✕ takes taps meant for «إضافة حركة».** `src/components/Toast.tsx:61` (`fixed inset-x-4 top-20`) puts the box at y 80–146 at 390 px; every R4 landing page has its action row at y 85–129 right there: owner ledger «إضافة حركة» (x 16–145) and «طباعة» (153–223), staff home and staff ledger «إضافة حركة», the employee page after a salary (`تعديل / كشف الشهر / كشف الحساب`, top 17 px). `pointer-events-none` does let a tap at the button's centre through (verified with `elementFromPoint` — `dbg2.mjs`), but the ✕ (`Toast.tsx:69`, `pointer-events-auto`, 44×44 at x 25–69, y 89–133) lies on the inline-end 44 px of «إضافة حركة», so a tap there dismisses the toast instead of opening the form, and for 3 s the owner sees no «إضافة حركة»/«طباعة» at all — the one thing R3 set out to stop. The R3 Playwright pin only checks «حفظ» on the two forms, where the row under the toast is the h1. Fix (smallest): render the ✕ only for `tone="error"` (a success toast dismisses itself; nothing in the strip then takes the pointer) and move the success box below the title row on phones — e.g. `SavedNotice`/`PaymentRecorded` could pass a `below` prop that becomes `top-36` — or mount `SavedNotice` in flow under the `<h1>` as a static `role="status"` strip (`mb-3`) instead of fixed, which also removes the 280 ms ride-in (N1). Either keeps `Toast` for errors as is. Test to add: at 390, after `/owner/transactions?saved=1`, the toast box does not intersect «إضافة حركة»/«طباعة» (and the ✕ intersects no `a`/`button`).

**S2 (SHOULD) — at 1280×800 the «حفظ وإضافة أخرى» toast overlaps the form's own buttons.** After the save the form stays scrolled to its buttons, which sit at y 757–801; the toast at `md:bottom-6` (`Toast.tsx:61`) spans y 710–776 and x 448–832, covering the top 19 px of «حفظ وإضافة أخرى» (675–824) and touching «حفظ» (832). Click-through works (`elementFromPoint` = the button, `dbg3.mjs`) and the ✕ (457–501) overlaps nothing, so it is visual only — but it is the R3 bug's desktop twin on the most common laptop viewport (the owner test said "not on desktop" because the form was shorter then). Fix: keep the toast under the top bar on md+ too (`top-20` without `md:top-auto md:bottom-6`; the desktop bar is also 69 px and the desktop page title row carries no buttons on the forms) — or `md:bottom-28`. Pin: at 1280×800 after «حفظ وإضافة أخرى» the toast box intersects neither «حفظ» nor «حفظ وإضافة أخرى».

**N1 (NOTE) — toast rides in with the page for ≈0.5 s.** `Toast` is a descendant of `#main > *`, whose `route-slide-in` (`src/app/globals.css:170–176`, `translate`) makes the slid element the containing block of the fixed toast while it runs: on `/owner/plans/{id}?paid=…` the toast measures `top: 165` until ≈500 ms, then `80` (`dbg.mjs`); on `?saved=1` landings the same for the first frames. Absent under reduced motion (fade only). Cosmetic; an in-flow `SavedNotice` (S1) removes it. Also: on the plan page the toast's bottom edge (146) touches the «محمد» link's top (145) by 1 px — nothing to do.

**N2 (NOTE) — native date inputs show `mm/dd/yyyy`** in this Chromium (UI locale, `lang="ar-SA"` does not change Chromium's picker text); the R13 caption «الأحد 11-10-2026» under every field is what carries the day-first reading. Expected; verify on a real iPhone as (b-14) asked.

Not part-3 regressions; listed for the backlog.
