## Task T2 — the toggle and SSR (C2, D11)
- `src/lib/theme.ts`: `type ThemeChoice = "light" | "dark" | "system"`; `parseTheme(cookieValue)` (anything unknown → `"system"`), `nextTheme(choice)` (light → dark → system → light), the cookie name `theme`, cookie attributes (path `/`, 1 year, `SameSite=Lax`, not HttpOnly — the client writes it), and `htmlThemeAttrs(choice)` returning the `<html>` attributes (`data-theme` absent for system, plus `style={{ colorScheme }}` or a class — your choice). Client-safe module (no `server-only`, no `next/headers`).
- `src/app/layout.tsx`: read the cookie with `cookies()` from `next/headers` and spread `htmlThemeAttrs(parseTheme(…))` on `<html>`. Every page is already dynamic (role layouts await `requireX()`); check the auth pages and `not-found` still build. `viewport.themeColor` may become a light/dark pair (`media: (prefers-color-scheme: …)`).
- `src/components/chrome/ThemeToggle.tsx` (client): sun/moon(/auto) icon button, 44 px, `aria-label={t.theme.toggle with {mode}}`, cycling light → dark → system; on click it writes localStorage (`zakham.theme`, try/catch) **and** the cookie, and sets/removes `data-theme` on `document.documentElement` at once (no reload). On mount it reconciles: if localStorage holds a choice and the cookie disagrees, the cookie wins (server truth) and localStorage is updated. Works for all three roles. White icon on the green bar (it sits in `TopBar`).
- Mounting: `nav` owns `TopBar.tsx` and adds `<ThemeToggle />` (D11). Tell `nav` the import path and props as soon as the component exists.
